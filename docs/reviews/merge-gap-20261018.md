# The temp→canonical merge gap: disposition

2026-10-18. Root cause, fix, and the tripwire that makes recurrence loud.

## What the hole was

Two crons did real work that never reached `data/congress.duckdb`, and both
exited 0 while doing it:

- **Weekly prices cron** (`data/logs/prices-cron/weekly.sh`): refreshed
  prices + dividends into a throwaway `prices.duckdb`, snapshotted it, and
  stopped. Four weekly DBs accumulated (through 2026-10-09), canonical prices
  stayed at 2026-10-15 only via manual refreshes, and the `dividends` table —
  which the ex-div entry gate (charter R9) reads — stayed at **0 rows**.
- **Daily Gemini OCR cron** (`data/logs/gemini-cron/daily.sh`): OCR'd
  zero-row PDFs into **September-era staging year DBs**
  (`data/.staging/rescrape/rescrape-20260921T224104/years/`, ~465 MB) that
  no step ever promoted. Canonical-side OCR already exists and works —
  `refresh --gemini-ocr` produced the Oct-15 `gemini_ocr` rows (e.g. the
  RPM filings) — so the staging path was redundant.

A process that writes artifacts nobody consumes is indistinguishable from a
broken one. Both holes were invisible because nothing checked.

## The plug

1. **`refresh_prices.py --merge-into CANONICAL`** — after the temp refresh,
   additively promote prices and dividends into canonical, restricted to the
   eligible universe of that refresh, inserting **only keys canonical is
   missing** (never overwrites/deletes; idempotent; `--merge-dry-run`
   reports without writing). The weekly cron now passes `--merge-into`, so
   the cron's work lands in canonical the same night it is produced.
2. **`scripts/doctor.py`** (daily cron 05:00 UTC,
   `data/logs/doctor-cron/daily.sh`) — fail-loud checks: price freshness vs
   the last completed NYSE session (limit 8 sessions), dividends populated,
   form4 feed ≤7d, receipt ledger ≤8d, and **no unconsumed cron artifacts
   older than 10d** — the exact signature of this hole, in either cron.
   Nonzero exit and a log line on any failure.
3. **Gemini staging cron retired** (crontab entry commented, reversible
   backup at `/tmp/opencode/crontab.backup-20261018`): its only output was
   unconsumed staging. Merging September staging parses into an October
   canonical would *regress* newer parses, so the staging root was moved to
   `data/archive/rescrape-20260921T224104-retired-20261018` (kept on disk,
   outside the doctor's active-staging scan); canonical OCR continues
   through `refresh --gemini-ocr`.

## Residual

- The four existing weekly temp DBs and the staging year DBs remain on disk
  (regenerable; the doctor flags them until they are pruned or consumed).
  The 20261012 temp was superseded by a fresh refresh+merge on 2026-10-18.
- Merging the September staging *parses* is deliberately NOT done: staging
  rows carry staging-era ingestion watermarks and older parse results.
- `scripts/doctor.py` checks are topology-aware but not exhaustive; any new
  cron that writes outside canonical must add itself to the doctor's
  artifact scan or wire its own merge.
